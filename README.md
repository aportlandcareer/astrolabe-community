# Astrolabe community

A place where Astrolabe members share what they make for each other and for us,
Dan and Danny, who build Astrolabe. Right now that's our first contest.

## The Thanksgiving mark contest

Our logo and wordmark are made of little nodes, and this Thanksgiving we'd love
to see what you'd make of them. Design a holiday version of the Astrolabe mark,
with one look for when your brain is resting and one for when it's thinking.
Maybe the globe turns into a pie with a lattice crust, the letters drift down
like autumn leaves while your brain thinks, or every node glows the warm orange
of candles on a long table. Silly, beautiful and strange entries are all
welcome.

**How to enter:** in Astrolabe, open Announcements (the megaphone at the top of
your page) and press **Make my entry**. You and your agent build the look
together on your own mark, and your agent sends it here as an issue when you're
happy with it. You can also open an issue yourself with the
**Thanksgiving mark entry** template.

**What you send:** a small CSS file for the mark, your handle, and, if you
like, a one-sentence message for every member and a link to something good for
the community (like a project you're building, a cause you care about, or a
free resource).

**The prize:** we ship the winning design to every member in our next release,
with the winner's handle, message and link beside it.

**Dates:** entries close Sunday, November 22, 2026, and we'll share the winner
the day before Thanksgiving.

## The small print, kept small

- Entries are CSS only and change nothing outside the mark, so anyone can try
  one safely.
- Links need to be community-minded: no ads, affiliate links, campaign
  fundraising or adult content. We'll ask you for another link if one doesn't
  fit.
- By entering, you're OK with us shipping your design in Astrolabe if it wins,
  credited to your handle.
- Be kind in comments. We're all here to make something lovely.
